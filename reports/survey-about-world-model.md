# Survey on World Models
## TL;DR
- World models can be categorized into types such as generative, predictive, and symbolic [1].
- Applications span autonomous driving, robotics, and healthcare, enhancing AI capabilities significantly [2].
- Limitations include issues of scalability, interpretability, and the need for robust evaluation benchmarks [3].
- Future directions are focused on integrating cognitive frameworks and enhancing physical prediction capabilities [4].

## Background
World models refer to the representations that agents create of their environments to make predictions and decisions. Foundational work indicates that the nature of these models is critical to various applications in AI and robotics, with generative and predictive models holding particular significance in simulations.

## Types of World Models
Different categories of world models have emerged, including generative models, which create simulations from scratch, and predictive models, which forecast future states based on current data [1]. Symbolic models also play a role by representing knowledge in structured forms, facilitating understanding [1].

## Applications of World Models
World models have widespread applications, particularly in fields such as autonomous driving where they assist in real-time traffic simulations, and in robotics where they enhance spatial intelligence and task execution [2]. Additionally, they are being applied in healthcare for robotic procedural training, showcasing their versatility across domains.

## Limitations and Challenges
Despite their advantages, world models encounter significant limitations, particularly regarding scalability and computational requirements. Interpretability remains a prominent issue, as many models fail to accurately represent complexity inherent in real-world scenarios, posing risks for unsafe predictions [3].

## Future Directions and Trends
Emerging trends indicate a shift towards creating more interactive models that utilize cognitive frameworks for decision-making in robotics and AI [4]. Enhancements in physical prediction capabilities and the establishment of scalable evaluation frameworks are critical challenges that need addressing to future-proof advancements in world modeling.

## Trends and Open Problems
As technology continues to evolve, the gaps between simulated environments and real-world applications persist. Ongoing research will need to focus on bridging these gaps while optimizing models for scalability and effectiveness [4].
